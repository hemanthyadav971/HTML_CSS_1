Our Heritage

Where Tradition Meets Contemporary Gastronomy

Founded in 1996 in the heart of Florence, Bella Tavola carries centuries of culinary legacy to your table. Every dish is crafted from heirloom recipes, cold-pressed olive oils, hand-milled flours, and fresh herbs harvest daily.

  • ✓ Authentic wood-fired oven shipped directly from Naples
  • ✓ Sommelier-curated wine cellar featuring 120+ vintage Italian wines
  • ✓ Dedicated handmade fresh pasta station visible from dining hall
